The Opportunity
This is an opportunity to build AI systems that solve real business problems.
At Transreport, there are many internal and external workflows that are still manual, repetitive, or slow.
We want to change that by building practical AI agents that improve productivity, reduce repetitive workload, and help teams operate more effectively.
As part of this internship, you will identify high-value use cases and build working AI agents that can automate or improve processes across the business. Examples could include:

AI agents for internal admin workflows across HR, Legal, Finance, or Operations

AI agents for service desk and support ticket handling

AI agents to support marketing execution, such as drafting LinkedIn posts or campaign content

AI agents for product, engineering, or QA workflows

AI tools that improve team efficiency and reduce manual effort

This is not a theoretical research project.

It is a hands-on build role focused on real deployment and measurable impact.
