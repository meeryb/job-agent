Description & Scope

Join a global software engineering team working on Omega, a large-scale enterprise platform comprising over 35 million lines of code and technologies spanning more than 15 programming languages. This internship offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience across software development, cloud technologies, automation, DevOps, quality engineering, and Artificial Intelligence. As an intern, you will collaborate with engineers across the UK, US, and India and contribute to real projects that support the development, testing, delivery, and operation of industry-leading scientific and engineering software used worldwide.

Skills

Throughout the internship, you will work on a variety of focused mini-projects, allowing you to explore different areas of software engineering while building practical skills in:

 Software development using Python, Java, C++, and scripting technologies.
 Automation of testing, deployment, and delivery processes.
 Cloud-native technologies and integrations with Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud Platform.
 Performance optimisation and reliability improvements for large-scale systems.
 Modern DevOps and Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) practices.
 Application monitoring, reporting, and quality metrics. Investigation and application of Generative AI technologies to improve engineering workflows.
 Development and prototyping of GenAI-powered capabilities, including intelligent automation, engineering productivity tools, and AI-assisted features integrated directly into the Omega platform. 
 Interns will gain exposure to both Windows and Linux environments, on-premises infrastructure, cloud-based systems, and modern software engineering practices used in large-scale commercial products.

The placement is designed to provide experience across the full software delivery lifecycle, from design and implementation through testing, deployment, and operational support.

Responsibilities

You will be fully integrated into the team's agile development process, participating in planning activities, daily stand-ups, technical discussions, and project reviews. Tasks will be selected according to business priorities and your interests and development goals.

 Designing and implementing automation solutions that improve software build, test, and release processes.
 Developing tools and services using Python, Java, C++, and scripting languages. Improving the robustness, performance, and reliability of engineering tools and workflows. Supporting the development of features within a large-scale scientific and engineering software platform.
 Contributing to cloud integration initiatives across Azure and Google Cloud environments.
 Creating dashboards, reporting solutions, and quality metrics to improve engineering visibility.
 Evaluating and applying Generative AI technologies to solve real engineering challenges.
 Prototyping and implementing AI-assisted capabilities that enhance user productivity and platform functionality.
 Collaborating with engineers across multiple global locations and technology domains.
 Learning and applying industry-standard software engineering, DevOps, and cloud development practices.
